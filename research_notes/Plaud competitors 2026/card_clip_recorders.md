## Q1. Prices, subscription tiers and free-plan allowances

### Takeaway
Plaud's 2026 lineup is Note (~$159), Note Pro ($189), NotePin (~$159), NotePin S ($179, CES 2026) plus the Plaud One earbuds ($249, Q4 2026); every Plaud device gets a 300-min/month free Starter plan, and Pro is $99.99/yr (1,200 min). ## Q3. Transcription accuracy/quality per independent reviewers

### Takeaway
Independent coverage (How-To Geek, Trusted Reviews, TechRadar, Notebookcheck, 9to5Mac, BGR, Cult of Mac, Digital Trends, SoundGuys) generally rates Plaud Note Pro around 90–98% accurate on clear speech with decent speaker labels in quiet rooms, degrading in noise/cross-talk and in non-English (Cantonese). Competitors are described as roughly at parity on standard English; speaker separation is the most common weakness. No head-to-head from Wired, The Verge, Wirecutter, CNET, PCMag or ZDNet was found in search (a ZDNet Comulytic review is referenced on Digg but not verified).

### Cited Findings
- Plaud Note Pro accuracy estimates across reviewers: ~95–98%; 95%+ in quiet; ~90–95%; drops off at ~5 m distance; struggled with Cantonese word accuracy and speaker differentiation; little background-noise reduction; speaker labels reasonably accurate in quiet 2–3-person chats, degrade with cross-talk; "Enhanced" mode occasionally clips words — summarised from [How-To Geek](https://www.howtogeek.com/plaud-note-pro-ai-recorder-notetaker-review/), [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/plaud-note-pro), [Coolsmartphone](https://www.coolsmartphone.com/2026/04/28/plaud-note-pro-review-the-credit-card-sized-brain-for-your-phone/), [The Tech Revolutionist](https://thetechrevolutionist.com/2026/01/plaud-note-pro-review.html), [mrktcorrect, 3/5] **[AFFILIATE?]**(https://mrktcorrect.com/blog/plaud-note-pro-review). (Search-summary attribution of each number to each outlet was not individually verified.)
- Plaud Note Pro also reviewed by Notebookcheck — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Review-of-the-Plaud-Note-Pro-AI-Recorder.1254695.0.html); original Plaud Note by TechRadar — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/plaud-note-review)
