# Plaud still wins, but rivals close in

*Research report, as of November 8, 2026. Prepared for a church operations pastor and part-time marketing director in Franklin, TN (iPhone 15 Pro, HubSpot, Google Workspace, wants Claude via MCP).*

## Bottom line up front

**Buy the Plaud Note Pro (about $159–$189) with the Pro plan ($99.99/yr). No competitor matches it on what you need most.** It is the only recorder with a **verified connector in Claude's directory, included on all plans** ([Claude marketplace](https://claude.com/marketplace/connectors/plaud); [Plaud pricing](https://www.plaud.ai/pages/plaud-ai-plan-pricing)). It is also the only one with a **native Zapier app** that can reach HubSpot and Google Drive ([Zapier](https://zapier.com/apps/plaud/integrations)), and the only one with an **announced SOC 2 Type II audit plus ISO 27001/27701 with a named auditor** ([Plaud blog](https://www.plaud.ai/blogs/news/plaudai-is-now-soc-2-type-ii-certified-heres-what-that-actually-means); [Plaud healthcare page](https://www.plaud.ai/pages/healthcare-solution)).

The serious rivals are **Pocket, Mobvoi TicNote, Fieldy 3, Anker Soundcore Work and Amazon's Bee Frontier**. Most of them win on price or free minutes, not on trust or integrations.

Plaud has two real weaknesses for confidential pastoral work. Its transcription is **cloud-only**, and press has reported **ties to a Shenzhen backer**. Neither rules it out, but both shape how you should use it (see the recommendation section).

The Limitless pendant is the warning case. **Meta bought the company, stopped sales overnight, and promised only "at least one year" of support**, which points to an end around December 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/meta-acquires-ai-device-startup-limitless/)).

## Rivals compete on free minutes, not trust

Hardware prices have settled into a **$119–$199 band**. The real fight is the subscription.

Plaud's 300 free minutes a month is now among the stingiest free tiers. Rivals offer more: TicNote 600 minutes, UMEVO a year of unlimited use, and Comulytic and Pocket "unlimited" (with caveats). Only Fieldy, at 150 minutes, gives less.

For a pastor recording weekly staff and board meetings, 300 minutes runs out fast. Plan on Plaud Pro ($99.99/yr for 1,200 min/mo) ([Asian Efficiency](https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/plaud-note-review/)).

Accuracy is close to a wash. Most devices send audio to similar cloud speech models. The differences that matter are **mic quality, speaker labeling and the app**.

Speaker separation is the most common complaint across brands. Plaud's labels hold up in quiet 2–3 person rooms and slip with cross-talk ([Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/plaud-note-pro)). 9to5Mac flagged the same issue on the Comulytic Note Pro ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/10/review-comulytic-note-pro-is-now-my-official-sidekick-for-any-meeting/)).

Plaud has a review-volume moat. Its iOS app holds about **4.9★ from ~26.8K ratings** ([Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/productivity/ai-plaud-ios-plaud)). Most competitors' reputations rest on vendor and affiliate posts.

Sources: [Plaud MCP docs](https://docs.plaud.ai/plaud-mcp-cli/mcp), [Claude marketplace](https://claude.com/marketplace/connectors/plaud), [Plaud Support: API](https://support.plaud.ai/hc/en-us/articles/60726890231449-How-can-I-get-API-access-to-my-Plaud-data), [Plaud Support: Zapier](https://support.plaud.ai/hc/en-us/articles/12200669941647-Zapier-integration), [HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t/plaud-notetaker/149994), [Omi MCP docs](https://docs.omi.me/doc/developer/mcp/setup), [Omi GitHub #20939](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/issues/20939), [Amazon hackathon resources](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/resources), [Fieldy blog](https://www.fieldy.ai/blog/introducing-fieldy-developer-platform-public-api-mcp).

Read-only access is a feature for confidential material. An AI agent connected to Plaud cannot alter or delete your records.

Plaud's lack of a public REST API matters only if you want custom n8n-style pipelines. For a HubSpot and Google Workspace user, the Zapier trigger ("Transcript & Summary Ready") covers the common jobs. It can send each summary to a Drive file and log it as a HubSpot engagement ([Zapier Plaud→Drive](https://zapier.com/apps/plaud/integrations/google-drive)).

Skip the community Plaud MCP servers. They rely on unofficial web APIs or a hijacked desktop session ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/mcp-server-plaud/)), and they add risk with no benefit now that the official one exists.

## Privacy: audited cloud beats unaudited promises

| Brand | Independent audit evidence | Data location / ownership | Main red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plaud** | SOC 2 Type II (Sept 2026), ISO 27001/27701 by Sensiba | AWS US-West per Plaud; US entity; reported Shenzhen backer | Cloud-only; China-link questions ([Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20250903PD210.html)) |
| **Pocket** | Claimed only | Delaware corp | Younger; claims unaudited |
| **Fieldy** | Claimed "independent audits"; no auditor named | US (Field Labs) | Stores no audio, so you cannot re-listen |
| **TicNote** | None found | HK-listed Chinese parent; US cloud claimed | Privacy "rests on trust" ([BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/articles/mobvois-4g-ai-earbuds-a-productivity-leap-or-a-privacy-trap)) |
| **Soundcore** | None | Anker (Chinese) | Anker admitted false Eufy encryption claims in 2023 ([MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2023/01/31/anker-eufy-camera-security)) |
| **Omi** | Claimed only | San Francisco startup | 14+ vulnerabilities reported April 2026; fix status unknown ([researcher](https://kasparovabi.github.io/security-research/2026-04-omi-disclosure.html)) |
| **Bee** | Trail of Bits review found 21 issues, fixed during the review | Amazon | Conversations can feed Amazon.com product suggestions ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazons-new-ai-devices-can-listen-to-conversations-and-suggest-things-to-buy/)) |

"End-to-end encrypted" in this category almost always means encrypted in transit and at rest. **The vendor's cloud still sees plaintext audio during transcription.**

Plaud says its AI subprocessors (OpenAI, Google, Microsoft) work under **zero-retention, no-training contracts**, and that it does not train on user data without opt-in ([Plaud Support](https://support.plaud.ai/hc/en-us/articles/57744162858009-How-your-data-is-used-in-AI-processing)). These are vendor statements, not independently verified.

HIPAA does not generally apply to pastoral counseling. Even so, a "HIPAA compliant" label on a consumer gadget means little without a signed BAA ([Mentalyc](https://www.mentalyc.com/blog/is-plaud-hipaa-compliant)).

**Tennessee is a one-party-consent state.** You may record conversations you take part in. Recording conversations you are not part of is a Class D felony ([RCFP](https://www.rcfp.org/reporters-recording-guide/tennessee/)). That makes always-on wearables like Bee and Omi a poor fit for a church building. Calls with people in all-party states such as California need everyone's consent.

The lesson from Limitless and Humane: **an acquisition can end service or move your confidential archive to a new owner's terms within days.** Plaud's revenue scale makes a shutdown the least likely outcome among these brands. Its size also makes it an acquisition target, though that is speculative.

It fits your setup point by point:

**Guardrails for pastoral and board conversations:**

| Practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Ask consent out loud, even though Tennessee is one-party | Protects trust; required for out-of-state callers in all-party states |
| Do not record counseling sessions by default | No cloud recorder in this market offers vendor-blind encryption |
| Keep counseling and marketing recordings in separate workflows | Do not route pastoral transcripts into HubSpot zaps |
| Export and delete on a schedule | Guards against a Limitless-style ownership change |
| Remember Claude is a second processor | Transcripts Claude reads through MCP pass through Anthropic as well as Plaud (inference) |

**Switch condition:** if the reported Shenzhen ties are a dealbreaker for your board, Pocket is the closest US-owned substitute. Accept that its security claims are unaudited and its free-plan terms are muddled.

## Source quality and research gaps

**Vendor-heavy evidence.** Many "Plaud alternatives" comparisons are written by competitors: Fieldy, UMEVO, Dymesty, HiDock, TicNote, MeetGeek, Comulytic, and Plaud itself. Pocket's pricing and Pro tier come partly from Plaud's own blog. Treat every battery, accuracy and certification claim marked [V] as unverified.

**Independent evidence is thin outside Plaud.** Plaud has reviews from How-To Geek, Trusted Reviews, TechRadar, Notebookcheck and Tom's Guide. Fieldy 3, UMEVO and Sandbar have no major independent hands-on review. Bee Frontier has essentially one.

| Gap | Impact |
|---|---|
| **No independent accuracy head-to-heads** (no word-error-rate tests from Wired, Verge, Wirecutter, CNET, PCMag) | Accuracy rankings are impressionistic |
| **Amazon ratings not verified** on amazon.com. Plaud's 4.5★ comes from a deal post; NotePin's 4.2–4.3★ from mirror sites | Treat star counts as approximate |
| Several reviews (BGR, 9to5Mac, TechCrunch) seen only as search summaries | Some outlet-to-number attributions not checked |
| Plaud's US plan prices not confirmed on plaud.ai directly | Prices may have shifted |
| Pocket's free plan contradicts itself across its own pages | Year-1 cost uncertain |
| No independent check of any vendor's data location | Plaud's "US-only storage" is a vendor claim |
| Omi vulnerability fix status unknown | Reinforces the avoid rating |
| Whether Limitless pendants have been shut off as of Nov 2026 | Unknown |

The overall picture is clear even with these gaps. Rivals have caught up on hardware and undercut Plaud on free minutes. None has matched its combination of audited security, a verified Claude connector, Zapier reach and financial staying power.
